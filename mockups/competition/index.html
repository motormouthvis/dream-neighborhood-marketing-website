Note A · Why this page exists and the home page table is gone Your instinct was right that the table was risky in the hero flow, but the reasoning matters for what we do next. Naming competitors on the home page creates awareness of alternatives for visitors who had none, and it invites price-shopping at the exact moment we want a single action. It also puts our credibility on numbers that can go stale without us noticing. On the other hand “they already know what they pay” is only true of the agent who is already a GreatSchools customer. Most solo agents have never seen an API price sheet, and a partner evaluating us against Local Logic definitely wants this. So: one anchor sentence on the home page, the full argument here, and a link between them. This page is also the right place to send a prospect mid-call.

Our free tier includes the ratings. So this is not only a cheaper option than their paid one, it is a more complete one.

A worked example

A brokerage site serving 100,000 listing views a month pays GreatSchools $607.50 on the School Quality tier, and still does not get the 1–10 ratings. At their 300,000 lookup ceiling it is $1,807.50.

The same brokerage pays us $0 for school data, with no view limit, and $99.92 a month if they also want the full Neighborhood Explorer across every agent.

Note C · Read the disclaimer block below carefully This is the part that makes a comparison page safe to publish. Comparative advertising is legal and normal, but it has to be accurate, current, and clear about what is being compared. The four paragraphs below cover: sourcing and a verification date, a commitment that prices change, an explicit statement that we are comparing named published tiers rather than whole companies, and a trademark and non-affiliation line. I would also put a calendar reminder to re-verify every quarter, because the risk here is not writing something false today, it is leaving something true today up for two years. If legal review is available before this goes live, this is the page to send them.

About this comparison

All third-party pricing shown on this page was taken from each vendor’s own publicly available pricing or licensing page and was last verified on 13 August 2026. Pricing and product features change without notice. Please confirm current rates and inclusions directly with the vendor before making a purchasing decision.

Comparisons refer to the specific named tiers described above and to the features those tiers include according to the vendor’s own published documentation. They are not a comparison of the vendors as a whole, and they do not account for enterprise, custom, negotiated, promotional or reseller pricing, which may differ substantially. Where a vendor does not publish pricing, we state that rather than estimate a figure.

Usage-based figures are illustrative calculations using each vendor’s published rates applied to a stated volume, and are not quotes. Your actual cost will depend on your traffic and your agreement with that vendor.
